Balance the beam · five-minute activity

1. Predict

A downward force of 8 N acts 0.30 m to the left of the pivot. A downward force of 12 N acts 0.15 m to the right. Which side will move down?

2. Test

Enter these four values in the simulator. Keep the forces and left distance fixed. Find the right distance that balances the beam. Then find a second balanced setup by changing a force as well.

Open the ConceptOrbit Balance the beam simulation

3. Explain

Use moment = force × perpendicular distance from the pivot. Explain how the smaller force can produce the larger turning effect.

Worked answers
  • The left moment is 8 × 0.30 = 2.4 N·m anticlockwise. The right moment is 12 × 0.15 = 1.8 N·m clockwise. The beam turns anticlockwise and the left side moves down.
  • The moments balance when the right load is 2.4 ÷ 12 = 0.20 m from the pivot.
  • Another balanced setup keeps 8 N at 0.30 m on the left and places 6 N at 0.40 m on the right. Both moments equal 2.4 N·m.
